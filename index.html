✦ Personal Note

A Letter to the Reader

A Note from Sumit

I am an observer of quiet moments and human emotion. Much of my time is spent listening to songs that carry unspoken memories, reading books that understand the tender complexities of life, and watching films that explore what it truly means to love, ache, and feel. I also hold a fond love for travelling, wandering through unfamiliar places and watching the world move at its own gentle pace.

By nature, I am someone who feels deeply and overthinks even more, navigating the silent spaces between questions and quietness. When thoughts become too heavy to hold inside, I turn to writing. This space is where those unspoken words finally find a place to rest.

- Sumit Sengar
