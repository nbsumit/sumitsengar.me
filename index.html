Behind the words

A little about me

I find a lot of myself in songs, books, and films. I like travelling, seeing unfamiliar places, and noticing the small things along the way.

I feel deeply and often think too much. When I struggle to say something out loud, I write. This is where I share those thoughts.

I hope you find something here that feels familiar.

- Sumit Sengar
